# DSH prompt 05 · Review, revise and render finals

Read the visual review criteria and verify the tool can see the images. Use a new independent reviewer session with `prompts/92-independent-visual-review.md` if the harness supports it. Ask it to inspect rendered proofs without your preferred coast or your original quality scores. If no independent visual review exists, record your own inspection method accurately and write a comparative contact sheet for the owner.

1. **First review · screen-size read.** Assemble equal-width thumbnails and ask where the coast starts, where the boat is, what place details differ and where the camera can travel. Also inspect the high-resolution cutaways for continuity and readability. Score all sites using the same criteria. Name specific defects and actions. Revise the highest-impact mismatches on each site. Re-render all affected comparable views.
2. **Second review · production read.** Compare before/after frames. Inspect seams, clutter, lost anchors, over-noisy water, repetitive planting, roof texture tiling, inconsistent scale, cutaway edges, lighting balance, and motion path. Fix each visible quality blocker you can reproduce. Keep full-size crops if the flaw disappears at contact-sheet scale. Certify only what was inspected.
3. Render all ten required 4K stills (three per coast plus the globe). Verify pixels, not just the Blender output property. Render the asset sheet and final parity sheet. Keep pictures free of decorative UI or scientific labels. If the cutaway looks scientific without context, let the handoff explain illustrative geometry and preserve a plain image suitable for future composition.
4. Write the review per site and update progress. Ensure investment in one location hasn’t made the other two look like rough drafts. Continue to export.
