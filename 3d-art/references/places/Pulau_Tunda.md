## Model from the references

- **From orbit:** foreground the shape of the sheltered landing and the changing shoreline before placing individual houses. Give the vegetation masses room to connect into a green interior with roof clusters opening around the landing. Avoid transplanting the tightly packed Lompo silhouette.
- **Near the landing:** use modest red and brown roofs, alternating pale concrete and planted edges, and a small group of original moored craft. Suggest mangroves on suitable stretches with sparse exposed roots near enough to read. Leave gaps along the shoreline and retain the small-boat scale.
- **For the cutaway:** carry a visible landing edge and the same boat to the new angle. A small muted nearshore habitat patch and a long clean waterline are enough to distinguish it from Lompo. Keep coral shapes abstract and the offshore water visually quiet.

**Leave illustrative:** extent or condition of reefs and mangroves, exact water color, measurement locations, pre-work case boundaries and depths. A shaded patch is an art cue, never an observed patch, field reading or restoration site.
