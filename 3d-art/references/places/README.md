Links are references to inspect. Only fold source imagery into deliverable textures when its entry explicitly clears reuse. Collected screenshots or illustrations in the rest of this pack have their own provenance. Measure colors from the completed CityRT audit and from the new Blender proofs. Color, season, shoreline, and lighting in any reference image can be date-specific.

Scope for this run: use imagery to inform visual forms and build manually authored local surfaces. It takes no ocean observations, classified water colors, measured terrain profiles, live maps, or chart data to produce these renders. The globe art remains a visual locator. Never put verified-sounding reef boundaries, dredging footprints, or survey measurements in the artwork.
