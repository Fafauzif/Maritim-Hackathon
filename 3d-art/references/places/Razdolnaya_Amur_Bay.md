## Model from the references

- **From orbit:** use a river opening, wide bay and low wetland margins to explain the location. Compose in bands from water channel to marsh to land. Keep the contrast between the inner inlet and bay gentle and the overall tonal range consistent with the other two coasts. Preserve enough connected water space for camera travel.
- **At field-view scale:** low shore grass, soft bank slopes, patches of scrub, subdued regional material accents, one compact survey boat. A hand-built working landing can anchor the camera if the art notes mark it as illustrative. The estuary's emptier land and larger water shapes still get deliberate detailing. Do not insert a city skyline to make the frame feel finished.
- **For the cutaway:** use a modest channel trough with long, sloping banks and a level water surface. Keep corresponding shore and boat anchors through rotation. Slight changes in water material are art direction. Keep the color transition broad and subdued so it will not read as a modeled sediment plume.

**Leave illustrative:** seasonal appearance, depth, exact shoals and channel conditions, river discharge and plume extent. The render depicts a visual reference for the estuary type, not a recreation of a documented flood or winter survey.
