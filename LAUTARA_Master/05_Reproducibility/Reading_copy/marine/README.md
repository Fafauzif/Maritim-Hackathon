# Marine and meteorological context for the Indonesian case replays

## Findings useful for decisions

1. **Keep regional transport and local exposure as separate decisions.** On the 27 October 2023 Tunda **inspection date** (no documented extraction or plume timestamp), Open-Meteo's Marine Best Match SMOC-derived current had a westward component in all 24 WIB hourly values (0.30–1.00 m/s total speed, velocity-weighted direction toward 276°). Copernicus Marine GLORYS12V1 on the UTC daily grid cell nearest Tunda (3.94 km from the island reference, 0.494 m layer) gave 0.85 m/s and 271° toward. All nine neighboring GLORYS grid centers at both sampled depths had westward components every day during the 31-day context window. Model agreement supports westward regional screening for this event. These methods share a Copernicus/Mercator modeling lineage, and the fixed cells, surface-drift components, and daily/hourly averaging differ. They cannot establish the actual extraction point, pathway, near-reef flow, sediment load or alert time.
2. **A low modeled sea-state does not settle the direction of exposure.** For the Tunda inspection day WIB, hourly significant-wave heights were 0.14–0.22 m (MFWAM) and 0.24–0.26 m (ERA5-Ocean). The first uses a sea-grid point around 2.7 km from the reference and the second about 42.5 km away. The models' circular direction difference (direction waves approach from) was 88° median across 24 paired values (24–133° range). MFWAM's native output time step is 3 hours, so API-provided hourly values are not 24 independent observations. Applying the deep-water relation L = gT²/(2π) only as a scale check gives ~20–32 m for MFWAM and ~18–20 m for ERA5-Ocean. Actual near-reef wavelength and resuspension need local depth and bed characteristics. Tunda's ERA5 grid had 0.0 mm rain on the WIB inspection day (0/2/1 mm in the three prior WIB days), with an event-day peak wind of 2.47 m/s (rank 29th from highest, across 31 days). NASA POWER's separate coarser MERRA-2-based estimate gives 0.23 mm on the same nominal UTC date. Modest weather estimates weaken the need to invoke a coincident major regional weather event and leave local rain, runoff, accumulated sediment, tidal resuspension, and other possible sources open.
3. **Screen candidate work windows over multiple months, grid cells and depths.** In Kodingareng's 274-day February–October 2020 case window, the nearest GLORYS grid point (2.55 km from the island reference) has an eastward component on 139/274 days at 0.494 m. February: 29/29 eastward, June: 4/30, July: 6/31, October: 22/31. The nearest 9.57 m cell has an eastward component on 230/274 days. Surface eastward fractions range from 110/274 to 189/274 across the surrounding nine cells, with opposing grid-cell signs on 92/274 dates. At 9.57 m, the nine-cell ranges and mixed-sign days are 189–239/274 and 94/274, respectively. A fixed direction is a poor screening assumption at Kodingareng. These numbers cannot establish operator safety limits, a suitable work window, official fishing grounds, or the direction of a past plume. February–October encompasses the documented case months, with activity unresolved on individual dates.
4. **Require timing corroboration before assigning “rain-led” or “dry” case labels.** At Kodingareng both atmospheric archives mark roughly equal numbers of UTC days at or above 5 mm (ERA5 63, MERRA-2 64), yet agree on 37 of these dates. Totals also differ (1,834.7 vs 1,162.0 mm), with descriptive daily Spearman rho 0.678. At Tunda over the 31 UTC dates ERA5 yields 26.7 mm and MERRA-2 yields 88.04 mm, agreeing on one ≥5 mm day out of five flagged by either. The estimates use alternative model grids, product lineage and point-selection rules. The 5 mm threshold is a sensitivity screen.

## Inputs and exact extraction

- Tunda reference point 5.81151° S, 106.27811° E, 12 October–11 November 2023 inclusive. Kodingareng reference point 5.14905° S, 119.26473° E, 1 February–31 October 2020. Reference points follow the prior LAUTARA case analysis and refer to island-area sampling, with dredging coordinates unresolved.
- Weather: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API with ERA5 and nearest grid cell, precipitation and 10 m winds. ERA5 grid ~0.25° (27.8 km latitude here), 1-hour data. NASA POWER daily API PRECTOTCORR requested with time-standard=UTC and checked using the response header. The NASA response identifies MERRA2 and POWER. The native meteorological grid is roughly 0.5° latitude × 0.625° longitude. UTC days are used for matched precipitation comparisons. Local WIB/WITA dates are identified explicitly in the case series, with UTC+7/+8 conversion and complete 24-hour bins. Figure 1 labels the available date convention per panel.
- Waves: Open-Meteo Marine API explicitly requested ERA5-Ocean (0.5° grid) at both sites and MeteoFrance MFWAM (0.08°, 3-hour native) at Tunda. Best Match current for Tunda, covering the SMOC era (since 2022), uses surface mean plus wave and tidal terms. API units confirmed m/s, wave m and wave period s. The Open-Meteo endpoint provides historical SMOC values for 2023 but does not provide a same-product 2020 comparison. Kodingareng wave data come from the coarser offshore ERA5–Ocean retrieval.
- GLORYS12V1: Copernicus Marine Global Ocean Physics Reanalysis GLOBAL_MULTIYEAR_PHY_001_030, public cmems_mod_glo_phy_my_0.083deg_P1D-m_202311 geo-chunked archive, daily 1/12° current estimates. Decode Blosc arrays using CF scale and fill metadata and verify nine neighboring grid centers × two depths nearest 0 and 10 m × 31 or 274 UTC days. The extracted depths are 0.494 and 9.573 m. Instantaneous tidal currents, sub-grid passages and near-bed transport remain unresolved. The 1/12° grid is about 9.2 km here. No interpolation is applied. The nearest cell and nine-cell sensitivity are both shown.
- Attribution: KKP 29 October 2023 bulletin documents the 27 October stop/inspection as a suspected marine spatial authorization violation. It gives the public reporting chronology. Kodingareng case months are contextual, with local operations documented in the Greenpeace/WALHI 2021 Spermonde report.

## Seven-year seasonal current benchmark

- Extended the GLORYS calculation to 1 January 2019–31 December 2025 inclusive. The acquisition contains 92,052 finite values (2 island regions × 9 cells × 2 layers × 2,557 UTC days) and the month–cell summary contains 3,024 rows. The benchmark uses the same extract, model grid, and sign rule as the case windows.
- At the nearest Tunda model grid (0.494 m), zero daily samples had an eastward component in October and November 2023. The same-month medians from the other six years were 22.6% in October and 40.0% in November. June–September medians were zero. On a 0.025 m/s neutral-band sensitivity, see the detailed summary. The baseline is retrospective and includes years after the case.
- At Kodingareng's nearest cell in June 2020, eastward days were 13.3% near the surface and 86.7% at 9.573 m. The other six years' June medians were 41.7% and 83.3%, respectively. In July 2020, the two case-layer fractions were 19.4% and 67.7% versus medians 22.6% and 69.4%. The seasonal tendencies and a coincidence of opposing modeled directions by depth support collecting depth-specific observations before claiming local pathways.
- Run python get_glorys.py --baseline to fetch daily data, then python analyze_current_baseline.py to produce glorys_month_cell_baseline.csv, marine_baseline_summary.json, and marine_current_seasonal.png. The analyzer accepts uncompressed or Gzip daily CSV. Use glorys_manifest_baseline.json for provenance.
