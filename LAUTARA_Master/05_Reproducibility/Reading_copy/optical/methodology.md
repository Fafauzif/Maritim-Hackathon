# Optical coverage methods, data and limits

## Decision

Treat a qualified image as a dated observation opportunity. A work-window decision needs an explicit fallback when the time or receptor is unobservable. More missions help ordinary revisit while cloud-correlated periods remain the binding constraint.

## Sources and compared geometries

- [Element 84 Earth Search STAC](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1) for Sentinel-2 legacy L2A COG catalog, 2019-01-01 through 2026-10-31 inclusive UTC. Main MGRS tiles 48MXU (Tunda) and 50MQV (Kodingareng Lompo). Legacy catalog defines the acquisition roster. When repeated processing variants share a site/day, prefer a public HTTPS quality-mask asset and stable ID order. Keep one record per date in the site tile. Same-platform, same-site, same-date Collection 1 QA assets within 2 minutes of recorded datetime served only as missing-read recovery. Collection 1 cannot replace the historical date roster: exemplar first quarters around Tunda include only 2 items in 2019 and 1 in 2022, while legacy returned 20 and 18 respectively including reprocess variants.
- [Microsoft Planetary Computer Landsat Collection 2 Level 2](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/landsat-c2-l2) for Landsat 8/9 OLI/TIRS, QA_PIXEL at 30 m, 2019-01-01 through 2026-10-31 UTC. The unsigned public asset URL is preserved in the inventory. A time-limited platform signing token was obtained from the public SAS endpoint and is not persisted. Treat multiple WRS scene products on the same UTC date as one date for opportunity after any product passes. No cross-sensor reflectance blending or mosaicking was applied.
- Target geometry is a fixed annulus of pixel centroids 1,500–3,000 m from Tunda (106.27811°E, 5.81151°S) or Kodingareng Lompo (119.26473°E, 5.14905°S), projected in each scene CRS. Typical full-ring area ~21.2 km². A ring is not a quarry polygon or exact fishery footprint. The numerator and denominator always use the same footprints. Fill is unqualified; pixels outside scene coverage remain unqualified or absent in the error record.
- Tunda reef overlay uses the same [UNEP-WCMC archival habitat geometry and metadata](https://data.unep-wcmc.org/datasets/1), object ID 7554, historical input recorded as 1999-2002 and unvalidated. The tested footprint is clipped inside 3 km of the Tunda anchor rather than constrained to the annulus. At 20 m, this gives 2,303 cell centroids. It is an example receptor-scale area, not a current coral-extent estimate.

## Raster quality flags and metrics

The main permissive common-purpose screen retains Sentinel-2 [Scene Classification Layer](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-processing) values {2, 4, 5, 6} at 20 m. Landsat [QA_PIXEL](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-quality-assessment-bands) retains pixels with none of bits 0–5 flagged as fill, dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud, cloud shadow, or snow. Strict comparisons use Sentinel-2 SCL class 6 only and Landsat qualifying QA pixels with bit 7 water set. Bit schemes and classifiers are sensor-specific, with different spatial resolutions and errors. These masks are opportunities for targeted visual or proxied optical inspection, not assured usability for turbidity retrieval. Glint, submerged substrate, optical absorption, haze and cloud edge effects may still interfere. SCL2 is allowed only in the broad screen.

At each pre-specified area, qualify an acquisition if at least 90% of cells qualify, with 80% and 95% sensitivity checks. The figures show detected coverage as date-level windows. Failed raster reads are explicit unknowns with lower/upper bounds on pass rates by treating the unknowns as all failing or all passing. Use 2025-01–2026-10 fully read paired periods for comparative revisit/gap claims. Whole-tile cloud cutoff uses metadata at ≤20% in the legacy counterpart for benchmarking. No organization is asserted to use these tiers or thresholds.

- Maximum unqualified run counts consecutive inclusive UTC calendar dates without any full-ring pass, including study-window boundary runs. Inter-pass elapsed interval including endpoints differs by one day. Full gap claims are only for a fully assessed date roster.
- Within 3/7 days = proportion of candidate calendar dates with a qualifying same-day or later acquisition within the horizon. In each matched window the final seven candidate dates are omitted to avoid right-censoring. This is a calendar opportunity statistic, not a probability of observing a transient event or an operational latency guarantee.
- Historic anchor dates only: named decisions and agency actions are source records. Satellite availability is a separate retrospective catalog check. The fact of a passing acquisition before/after does not establish contemporaneous product availability or confirm quarry location.

## Files

- `sentinel_longitudinal.csv`, `landsat_longitudinal.csv`: acquisition-level records including unqualified/missing rows, representative raster URLs, two QA fractions, and Tunda historical reef mask fractions.
- `annual_sentinel_summary.csv`, `monthly_sentinel_summary.csv`, `multisensor_summary.csv`: denominator and threshold-sensitive counts, full uncertainty envelope and calendar gaps.
- `paired_archive_qa.csv`: same-date/platform comparison across both S2 archives when both masks were read.
- `optical_event_window_summary.csv`, `optical_event_nearby_inventory.csv`: historic availability by dated case anchor.
- `run_coverage.py`, `run_sentinel_collection1.py`, `run_landsat_history.py`: STAC discovery and GDAL range-read extractors. Recovery scripts retry failed initial assets. `assemble.py`, `summarize.py`, `historical_windows.py`, `plot_pilot.py`: scripts to reproduce the summaries and PNGs. Use Python with GDAL bindings and numpy for retrieval and pandas and matplotlib for postprocessing. Sensor collection phase files use shared historical filenames. Individual failing URLs are marked in CSV. Discovery caches are refreshed by running in a clean directory. The published raster quality percentages can be verified against the unsigned COG paths and the fixed geometry above without trusting the summary table.
