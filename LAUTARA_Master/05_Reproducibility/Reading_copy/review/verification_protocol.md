# Independent verification protocol

## Decision before method

The article earns a prevention implication only when it identifies an actor, a target receptor, a decision time, a credible opportunity to do something different, and the specific evidence that would differentiate the options. Real decisions differ in burden of proof. Requiring a baseline map before work, voluntary precaution, a public authority’s field inspection, a formal suspension, and a sanctions finding each have different evidentiary and authority requirements.

## Assertions to reproduce

1. Parse the embedded scene tables directly from the previous report and recalculate 20% tile vs 90% local water-class passes and calendar gaps. Match site, footprint, time windows, classification and cutoff first. Record differences in new results instead of overwriting prior figures. Keep classified-water screening separate from valid aquatic reflectance.
2. For the extension, audit both scene identifiers and site-days, especially two platforms or reprocessed versions of one acquisition. Same-day Landsat and Sentinel are separate observations but only one union calendar day. HLS derived from those sensors does not add a new overpass. For numerator and denominator, distinguish query returned, target covered, classification read, usable-by-rule, and interpreted manually. Missing catalog items and unread rasters get unknown status.
3. Compare matched months/site-era in the full series and a genuinely independent later holdout period. For variable sensor coverage or acquisition policy, mark year-specific mission availability, operational interruptions and incomplete months. Seasonal variation in valid scene counts can mix cloud, sampling, footprint and archive attrition. A gap between passes is different from a censored window edge. Express time in UTC and local zone if dates can change. Kodingareng local reporting is WITA, Tunda WIB, the Amur Bay area uses Vladivostok time.
4. Check the location every number means. Geometries need source, vintage, CRS, full vs intersection footprint, independent role (reef receptor, fishing-ground receptor, operation/permit polygon, ring or scale illustration), and data support. Reject any implication the island-centered ring traces mining or a current reef footprint. Check coastline or shallow bottom artifacts against manual profiles and repeated scenes.
5. Reproduce key counts, release-clock relationships and joins with an independent process. Different metadata catalogs for the same scene test inventory and lineage, not full triangulation. Shared input products and literature drawing on one press release stay one evidence cluster.

## Event replay tests

Use one row per evidential observation, a temporal precision field, separate onset/report, sensor acquisition and known earliest-public-availability fields, and verified source URL. Date-only events remain intervals. Match on site and receptor. A later retrospective product can inform mechanism, not contemporaneous response. An absent historical dissemination stamp stays unknown. Replay with (a) strictly documented by a conservative decision cutoff, (b) availability-unknown flagged separately, and (c) retrospective context. Explicitly propagate date-window uncertainty and keep a missing observation blank. Never convert absence of a public report into activity absent, operator compliance, or absence of response. If permit polygon available, check historical legal vintage and referenced activity before overlaying status.

For 27 Oct 2023 the action date is documented and onset and operating coordinates remain unverified. Present the 29 Oct press release as a later public document of the action. A prior image is an earlier scene rather than a clean no-activity baseline. Boskalis’s Feb–Oct 2020 project statement documents a broad work window, rather than daily activity in all cells. “Rainfall-led reference” means an exposure scenario with meteorological support. It cannot be a confirmed non-dredging negative unless independent coverage is adequate.

## Attempts to reverse interpretation

- Recompute if an alternate reasonable screen or local footprint materially changes the direction. Report both benefit and cost of added dates, such as apparent clear but unclassifiable images, changes in longest transparent gap, or scenes still obscuring a particular reef.
- Consider a regional rain or wave explanation using season-matched comparators and different antecedent aggregation lengths. Point NASA IMERG or ERA5 cells are exposure proxies, not river discharge, seabed disturbance, or observed runoff paths. GPM IMERG Final, backfilled reanalysis and present harmonized datasets enter the retrospective view where their day-of availability is unverified.
- Use optical QA, false-color reference and regions of contrasting depths. Repeated optical anomalies are not automatically recurring sediment, and SPM retrieval is not concentration without applicable calibration. Cloud-edge, sunglint, land adjacency, shallow bottom, bloom and mask behavior remain rival mechanisms.
- Use at least two controls where feasible: same-region upstream or opposite-sector and an environmental counterexample. If acquisition quality varies, differences in observability are a competing explanation for apparent recurrence. A model trajectory is not an observation. No reef ranking from a regional current grid when cells straddle the island or relevant features. For illustrative vectors, report cell size, nearest valid cell/distance, temporal aggregation, advection-only nature, and perturbation ranges.
- Add data layers sequentially, showing any change to a permissible decision explicitly. A hypothetical decision rule is neither a calibrated ecological threshold nor an actual performance measure without labeled events and independent tests. Mark an ambiguous or intermediate state rather than silently classifying it as no risk.
- An international contrast can challenge a portable method, not identify Russian and Indonesian source equivalence. Winter ice, shallow bottom, tidal/wind conditions, mask/version differences and boundary geometry can affect Amur.
- A measurable root cause must link what changed, affected mechanism, relevant time, and a defensible alternative. Where one link is unavailable, use a bounded claim such as “document capture date before treating a feed as contemporaneous” or “condition scheduling studies on site-scale transport evidence.”

## Handoff acceptance

Verify page count, links, legible chart axes and denominators, chart-to-CSV parity, archived URLs/IDs, and reproducibility from working folder and a clean extract. Bundle should state which inputs are mirrored and which are downloaded, expected versions, relative paths, code entry points, and what requires unauthenticated HTTP versus Earthdata login. Verify material statements across intro, figures, conclusion, and metadata for false precision or stronger verbs. Check summary labels “measured,” “observed classification,” “reported,” “proxy,” “modeled scenario,” and “proposed rule” at their point of use. Red-team only claims decisive to the pitch and rerun fixes before final.
