CHAPTER 1 
ABOUT LAUTARA 2026 
 
1.1. Background 
As an archipelagic nation, Indonesia's identity, economic development, and 
strategic interests are deeply connected to the maritime sector. The ocean is not 
merely a geographical boundary; it is a vital source of livelihood, economic 
opportunity, environmental sustainability, connectivity, and national security. 
 
Similarly, Russia possesses significant maritime interests and capabilities, with a 
long-standing history in maritime development, ocean governance, scientific 
research, and maritime security. In an increasingly interconnected world, maritime 
challenges require international cooperation, innovative thinking, and meaningful 
participation from younger generations. Issues such as marine environmental 
degradation, sustainable maritime development, maritime connectivity, ocean 
governance, and maritime security demand collaborative approaches that 
transcend national boundaries. 
 
Recognising the importance of youth participation in shaping the future of maritime 
affairs, LAUTARA 2026: Russia-Indonesia Maritime Hackathon is initiated as a 
platform for young people to exchange perspectives, explore innovative ideas, and 
develop solutions addressing contemporary maritime challenges. 
 
Organised by Russian House Jakarta, in collaboration with Maritim Muda 
Nusantara and Generasi Energi Bersih (GenB) Indonesia, LAUTARA 2026 
brings together young people from different institutions and backgrounds to 
contribute to a shared vision of a more sustainable, innovative, and secure maritime 
future. 
 
1.2. Commemorating Hari Nusantara 
LAUTARA 2026 is held in commemoration of Hari Nusantara, a national occasion 
that celebrates Indonesia's identity as an archipelagic nation and recognises the 
strategic importance of its maritime territory. 
 
This commemoration serves as a meaningful reminder that Indonesia's future is 
inseparable from the ocean. It also provides an opportunity to encourage young 
generations to understand maritime issues, strengthen their awareness of ocean-
related challenges, and actively contribute to sustainable maritime development. 
 
Through LAUTARA 2026, Hari Nusantara is translated into a platform for 
international dialogue, youth innovation, and cross-cultural collaboration.

1.3. Russia-Indonesia Youth Cooperation 
Indonesia and Russia have opportunities to strengthen their engagement in 
maritime-related areas through knowledge exchange, youth collaboration, 
innovation, and mutual understanding. 
 
LAUTARA 2026 provides a space for young Indonesians to explore maritime 
cooperation between the two countries, exchange perspectives, and propose ideas 
that may contribute to future collaboration. 
 
By bringing together students, youth communities, NGO representatives, and 
Indonesian Navy cadets, the programme aims to foster an inclusive environment 
where different perspectives can contribute to constructive maritime dialogue. 
 
1.4. Objectives 
LAUTARA 2026 aims to: 
a. Encourage young generations to understand contemporary maritime 
challenges and opportunities. 
b. Promote innovative and sustainable solutions for maritime development. 
c. Strengthen youth engagement in Russia-Indonesia maritime cooperation. 
d. Provide an international platform for knowledge exchange, collaboration, and 
constructive dialogue. 
e. Develop the leadership, analytical, communication, and problem-solving 
capacities of young participants.

CHAPTER 2 
GENERAL REGULATIONS & PARTICIPANT ELIGIBILITY 
 
1.1. Eligibility Criteria 
LAUTARA 2026 is open to young Indonesians who are interested in maritime 
affairs, sustainability, international cooperation, innovation, and maritime security. 
Applicants must fulfil the following requirements: 
- 
Be Indonesian citizens. 
- 
Be between 16 and 25 years old at the time of registration. 
- 
Participate as part of a team consisting of 4–6 members. 
- 
Represent the same institution, university, community, NGO, or Indonesian 
Navy cadet institution. 
- 
Each team must appoint one representative as the Team Leader. 
- 
The Team Leader will serve as the primary point of contact between the team 
and the Organising Committee 
- 
Have an interest in maritime issues and the overall theme of LAUTARA 2026. 
- 
Be able to communicate and present ideas in English. 
- 
Submit all required registration documents and the video assignment within the 
registration period. 
 
1.2. Eligible Institutions and Categories 
Participants may register under one of the following institutional categories: 
A. University / Higher Education Institutions: Students from the same 
university or higher education institution. 
B. Youth Communities: Members of the same youth community or organisation 
with an interest in maritime affairs, sustainability, or international cooperation. 
C. Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs): Young representatives or 
members of the same NGO working in relevant areas. 
D. Indonesian Navy Cadets (TNI AL): Cadets representing the same Indonesian 
Navy educational or cadet institution. 
 
1.3. General Participation Rules 
- 
Each participant may only be registered as a member of one team. 
- 
Each team must submit one complete registration form and one video 
assignment. 
- 
All submitted information must be accurate and verifiable. 
- 
Teams must ensure that their submitted work is original and does not violate 
intellectual property rights. 
- 
Plagiarism, fabricated information, or misrepresentation of participant identity 
may result in disqualification. 
- 
The Organising Committee reserves the right to verify participant eligibility and 
registration documents. 
- 
Only six selected teams will proceed to the final stage and receive an invitation 
to participate in the in-person hackathon at Russian House Jakarta.

4.1. Overview 
The video assignment is a mandatory component of the initial selection process. 
Through this assignment, participants will have the opportunity to demonstrate their 
understanding of contemporary maritime challenges, their ability to analyse 
relevant issues, and their capacity to communicate ideas in an international setting. 
Each team must submit one video assignment as part of its online registration. 
 
4.2. Main Question 
All teams must respond to the following question: 
 
"What do you think is the most important challenge facing maritime affairs 
today, and why?" 
 
The response should reflect the team's understanding of maritime affairs and 
demonstrate a thoughtful perspective on why the selected challenge deserves 
attention. Teams are encouraged to connect their discussion to the main theme or 
one of the LAUTARA 2026 sub-themes.

4.4. Recommended Video Structure 
To help teams prepare a clear and compelling submission, the following structure 
is recommended: 
A. Part 1 – Introduction 
Team name; Institutional affiliation; Names of team members; Selected 
maritime issue or challenge. 
B. Part 2 – Understanding the Challenge 
Explain the maritime challenge your team considers most important. What is 
the issue?; Where or how does it occur?; Who is affected?; Why does it 
matter?. 
C. Part 3 – Analysis and Perspective 
Present your analysis of the issue. Consider explaining: The underlying 
causes; Its current and potential impacts; Why existing approaches may be 
insufficient; Its relevance to Indonesia, Russia, or the wider maritime 
community. 
D. Part 4 – Proposed Direction or Solution 
Share your initial perspective on how the challenge could be addressed. You 
may introduce: An innovative idea; A potential solution; A collaborative 
initiative; A possible role for young generations. 
E. Part 5 – Closing Statement 
Conclude with a concise statement explaining why your chosen issue 
deserves attention and why young people should be involved in shaping the 
future of maritime affairs. 
 
Note: The recommended structure is a guide, not a mandatory script. Teams 
may develop their own creative presentation format while ensuring that the 
main question is clearly answered 
 
4.5. Language and Presentation Guidelines 
All video content must be delivered in English. Participants are expected to: 
- 
Use clear, understandable, and appropriate English. 
- 
Communicate ideas in a structured and logical manner. 
- 
Demonstrate confidence and clarity when presenting. 
- 
Use relevant examples, facts, or references where appropriate. 
- 
Ensure that all spoken content is understandable. 
- 
Participants do not need to use highly technical vocabulary. The priority is to 
communicate ideas effectively and demonstrate a sound understanding of the 
selected issue.
